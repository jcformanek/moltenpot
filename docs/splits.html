Molten Pot — Setting 3 splits

Setting 3 trains a single offline policy on a subset of a substrate's scenarios and evaluates it on the disjoint complement. Each card below is one such split: trained-policy rollouts on the train scenarios appear at the top, then a downward shift arrow signals the distributional move to the held-out test scenarios below. The clips are end-of-training PPO rollouts on each scenario — a reference for what an "expert" focal policy looks like in that scenario. Each card closes with a per-algorithm plot of the normalised final-eval return, with a circle for the in-distribution mean and a square for the out-of-distribution mean (error bars are SE; faint markers behind each mean are the individual (scenario, seed) samples).
